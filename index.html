WatchRace

A top-down racing game for Apple Watch and iPhone.

Privacy PolicySupport

Steer your car with the Digital Crown on Apple Watch, or with the on-screen buttons on iPhone, and finish each track in time. 120 tracks in 12 worlds, four special races, coins, stars and a garage full of paints, neons, bodies and upgrades.
